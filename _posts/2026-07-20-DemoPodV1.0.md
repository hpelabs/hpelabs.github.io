Live demo Environment – HPE Value added management tools

# Live demonstration environment
## The Purpose

The purpose of this demonstration environment is to show off our HPE Management tools.

During the demonstration, you will connect to **HPE GreenLake** and **HPE Compute Ops Management** and access several different **iLOs**. We are continuously expanding the capabilities available in this environment.

The environment currently includes **HPE Synergy Gen12** and **HPE OneView** systems. We are also planning to bring back our demonstration of **Thales CipherTrust Manager**, with more updates to follow in that area.

Please check this **Demo Guide** regularly for the latest information on supported demonstrations and newly added capabilities. It is available at [https://hpelabs.github.io/DemoPodV1.0/](https://hpelabs.github.io/DemoPodV1.0/)

## Get Connected

You will connect to a Omnissa Horizon environment. With your browser (Chrome recommended) connect to the following URLs based on your present location:

- Outside of an HPE office use: [https://eap.compute.cloud.hpe.com/](https://eap.compute.cloud.hpe.com/)

- Inside an HPE office use: [https://eapcompute.its.hpecorp.net/portal/](https://eapcompute.its.hpecorp.net/portal/)

With:
- Username: Sent to you by email
- Password: You set after initial login

### Procedure

1. After clicking one of the links above, you will be presented with the "Your connection isn’t private" prompt, click on **Advanced**.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG001.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG001.png){:class="img-800"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

2. Click on the link to Continue to your IP address. This is not an "unsafe" thing to do in this case.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG012.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG012.png){:class="img-600"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

3. At the Horizon client selection screen, choose on the right side of the login box, **Omnissa Horizon Web Client**.
  
    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG023.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG023.png){:class="img-500"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

4. At the Horizon Login screen, enter the username sent to you in email and the password you initially set and click on **Login**.

5. When successfully logged in you are presented with a clickable link that launches your demo station. This is where you will work. Make sure you stay within the VPN connection when using a browser. If you connect to a site via your own browser, you will not have a network connection to the lab. You must stay within the Horizon terminal. Click on the **Demo Envir...** link.
 
   [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG034.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG034.png){:class="img-300"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

6. We are now going to launch some of the demo environment. We are going to log into GreenLake from your demo desktop. First we have to initiate Chrome. Click on **Chrome** in your System Tray.

7. When Chrome loads, you may have to initially set it up. Don't sign into Chrome, stay Signed Out.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome.png){:class="img-700"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

8. Skip the setting to set Chrome as the default Browser.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome1.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome1.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

9. Launch HPE GreenLake at greenlake.com. If the browser warns that the link is unsafe, it is not unsafe. Proceed to GreenLake with the Launch GreenLake button.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome2.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome2.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

10. When prompted to login, use your HPE email credentials.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome3.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome3.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

11. At this point, HPE Okta starts. Allow **mylogin.hpe** and then get a Push Notification from Okta.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome4.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome4.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

12. For the Workspace in GreenLake choose, **Dream Company**

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome5.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome5.jpg){:class="img-300"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

13. After login, choose the Compute Ops Management that is focused on US West.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome6.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome6.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

14. Notice the What's new pop-up that lets the user know what has recently been deployed in the environment. Click on the "x" to close the pop-up.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome7.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome7.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

15. You are now in the Compute Ops Management view of Dream Company. Remember this is a live environment! No simulator.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome8.jpg){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/Chrome8.jpg){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

16. Please logout of the Horizon environment when you are done. To do this, close the Chrome browser instance you have been using.

17. Now click the three vertical bars on the left side of your VPN connection and navigate to **Log Out**. You can see the three vertical bars in the graphic below.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG048.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG048.png){:class="img-900"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}

16. You are looking for the section to **Log out**. Click on the link to successfully log out.

    [![]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG002.png){: .bordered-image-thin}]( {{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/HOLs/PresentationDemo/IMG002.png){:class="img-500"}{: data-lightbox="gallery"}
